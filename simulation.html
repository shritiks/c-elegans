How the model behaves

  • If unc-47 is higher than both eat-4 and odr-10, inhibition dominates: the worm ignores stimuli and wanders, changing direction frequently.
  • Otherwise it moves toward the odour source or favourable food. When both are present, it chooses food if eat-4 exceeds odr-10, and the odour otherwise.
  • glr-1 sets crawling speed and how often the worm changes heading while exploring.
  • The worm turns away from an unfavourable food source when it gets close.
